# Explicitly authorized legacy copyediting

This reference applies ONLY when this run explicitly authorizes copyediting.
Mechanical-only plans contain no flights, merge-desk, or wave-2 reread line;
mechanical sweeps/number audit/continuity and the mandatory rotated Verify are
not copy-edit lanes. Never reopen forbidden lanes through a genre preset.

Run mechanical work first and verify it before `/flight-deck` reads the already
proofread text. The six lenses are economy, word_choice, flow, clarity, rhythm,
repetition; decomposition and proposer diversity preserve recall. A singleton
proposal is not rejected merely for lacking agreement. Keep the approved matrix,
posture, and budget. Use subscription Claude proposing/judging; a paid route
must be explicit, and Claude must never fall back to the Anthropic API.

Copyedit-wave permits style only where genre allows; external-judgment proposes
without auto-applying. Posture is separate from stage/approval: house flights
are lenient with hard vetoes, while literary/memoir and academic packs can be
strict. Reject meaning/emphasis shifts, dialect/idiolect/coinages/voice changes,
deliberate-fragment or rhetorical-repetition changes, lateral swaps, and
worsenings. Under lenient posture a defensible marginal improvement remains a
rejectable offer to the author. Mechanical findings always use STRICT judgment.

Use `/adjudicate`, then `/merge-desk` for authorized cross-lane work. Mechanics
win overlaps. A subsuming rewrite is possible only when the merge tool's policy
permits it, contains the mechanical correction verbatim, and sweeps plus LT prove
the contested span clean; unavailable LT is not a clean result. Preserve cluster
atomicity, one owner per span, and the two correct tracked-change authors.

Re-read only hypotheses whose chapter × error-class scope and cost were approved
in PLAN.md or a recorded reply. Price the exact command and honor the marginal
cost ceiling (default $2 per additional finding); do not launch an unapproved
wave because an audit raised an idea. Keep judgment prompts byte-stable for
caching; use approved batch routing when suitable. Historical flight estimates
undershot dense books: the exact dry-run is the pricing authority, not a
blanket old multiplier or a historical rate. Independently verify the resulting
build again, settle, certify, and follow `references/delivery.md`.
