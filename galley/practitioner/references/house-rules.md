# Editorial and house rules

Apply these within the common policy's genre, scope, intent-zone, and detected
English-variant constraints. They are the current rules distilled from the
Redding, Purpura, Paik, and Georgis precedents; full examples remain in history.

## Chicago mechanics and protected voice

- Spell numbers through one hundred; use numerals above one hundred. House clock
  form is `4:00 AM` / `11:00 AM`, and percentages are `40 percent`. Equivalent
  number/time formatting is mechanics; changed values are facts. Leave stage,
  type, grade, and display labels protected from ordinary number conversion.
  Chapter/part label numbering and case/style drift are the explicit exception:
  renumber continuously in the dominant form as tracked heading edits, note once.
- Use unspaced em dashes, house ellipses, punctuation inside quotation marks,
  and single quotation marks nested inside dialogue. TV shows take quotes;
  `title_italics` must not italicize them. Apply house forms consistently without
  mistaking them for errors in verify/settle. Preserve quoted Scripture/liturgy/
  historical wording under its zone permission.
- Use serial commas when items are already comma-separated; `A and B and C`
  is intentional polysyndeton, not a serial-comma error. A compound predicate
  takes no comma before `and`. Correct comma splices with semicolon or period,
  never by merely deleting the comma. Vocative commas, action-beat periods,
  grammatical agreement, and tense are mechanical even when a gate held them.
- Under the book's US variant, preserve the measured house corrections for
  towards/amongst/-wards and generic university/pharmacy school/stadium capitals.
  Proper institutions/names and declared capitalized terms remain protected;
  do not Americanize a detected British book or lower an intentional name.
- Do not rewrite a dangling modifier or gerund into a clause as line editing
  under a mechanical brief. Query only when actor attribution genuinely needs
  author knowledge. Do not make unsupported meaning changes to pronouns/direction
  words. `a.m.`/`p.m.` and `five-hundred-pound` must not undo the house time/number
  forms. Protect deliberate fragments, rhetorical repetition, dialect, coined
  language, character voice, interior monologue, and intentional tense exceptions.
- Protect attribution/copyright template/display lines structurally before the
  ladder. Use locked intent zones for label formatting so number/period passes
  cannot damage it; explicit chapter-label repair rows still follow the authorized
  label map. On split-paragraph books disable the destructive terminal-period
  sweep before approval. Never detect headings solely by style name.

## Decide before querying

Read the WHOLE sentence with the proposed edit applied; reject `standing silent`
→ `stood silent` if it would create `was stood silent`. Check wider context where
meaning depends on it. A surface match alone is never proof of an inconsistency.

- A verbatim sentence or >=5-word passage accidentally repeated in this paragraph
  or its neighbor: delete the second copy tracked; preserve rhetorical repetition.
- A compound Merriam-Webster closes: apply the dictionary form, e.g. washcloth,
  coffeehouse, courthouse, chickenpox, drugstore, haystack, pincushion. Do not
  blindly merge an unknown compound; check dictionary evidence. Known hyphen drops
  include handcrafted, secondhand, nonnegotiable, posthaste, checkups.
- A pronoun unambiguously established by the sentence, or a direction word
  contradicted by its context: edit tracked. Joint possession, `ladies' man`,
  conditional past perfect, and agreement such as `were sent` are mechanics when
  supported by the sentence. Stray leading spaces and equivalent `IL`/`Illinois`
  or `$3.00`/`$3` styling follow the approved house rules, not a fact change.
- Names/numbers/titles/quoted facts without decisive evidence: author knowledge
  is required. Do not invent an answer. Missing dictionary data, a tool failure,
  or an unanchorable repair remains internal work, not an author question.
- Vet every spelling-variant pair in both contexts: homographs, different senses
  or parts of speech, names versus common words, and longer phrases may both be
  correct. Drop those false inconsistencies. Preserve MW-accented words and
  protected nouns; verify accents/loanwords against actual meaning and names.

Keep one counted comment for a same-rule family; remove a second comment on the
same unresolved question/span. Distinct questions in the same sentence stay
separate. Each surviving comment asks one specific question plus one sentence of
evidence (e.g. surname alternatives and their counts). Never forward a grammar
diagnosis, a suggested "Query the author" replacement, an already resolved premise,
or a note that a number was simply left unchanged. Preserve original author
comments during reconciliation; comments must bind to the final file's hash.

## Screening and replay safeguards

Every row, including deterministic sweeps, spellscan, and LanguageTool, is audited.
LT real-word replacements stay disabled by default; punctuation/spacing/casing/
en-dash findings still need screening. Do not de-accent a word the dictionary
accents or bypass the protected-noun allowlist. On voice-heavy prose the approved
plan may leave LT off. A suggested word change is not automatically a justified
margin question: apply the decide/drop/query rule above.

One owner per span. Composites/repair clusters are atomic; changing one member
withdraws the cluster unless it is replaced with independently validated rows.
Do not splice editorial notes into replacement text. Earliest identical finds
win; dedupe insertions by point and suppress duplicate queries. Curated/imported
rows never seed recurrence. Concordance a new catch across its whole construction
family and file every confirmed site through `references/findings.md`.

A truncated/unread result is a coverage loss, never clean evidence. Dense windows
need bounded output and files; do not reduce coverage or raise confidence just to
fit a response. Rebuilt text needs fresh verification bound to the actual build;
stale pre-rebuild artifacts are not proof. Correct held mechanics may be promoted
only after checking the sentence yourself and replaying through the engine.
