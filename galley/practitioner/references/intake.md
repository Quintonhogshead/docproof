# Intake evidence — profile and priced plan

Fresh book workspace; keep reusable house precedents but never another book's
scratch state. Open intake with source/config hashes and verify hashes on resume.
Use `/profile` then `/draft-plan`; this stage spends $0 and stops at the gate.
Read `references/config.md` when constructing/pricing the proposed run, not after
approval. Read `references/findings.md` only when authoring zone/import files.

Run structural inventory, chapter/part-label map, proper-noun counts, tic counts
with 2–3 samples, intent-zone selectors, and the required number extraction to
files. Every numeral AND spelled number (including ordinals, half/dozen and larger
magnitudes) gets its actual para_id and context. Tag age/date/year/time/sum/count/
distance/other; record numbers{count,by_kind,file}. The number audit examines
EVERY hit for house style, consistency, and arithmetic. Facts become queries;
chapter/part labels become tracked sequence/style corrections noted once.

Run `docproof tense` on EVERY book. Establish tense + person + declared exceptions
from above, not merely the previous sentence; a historical-present scene looks
internally consistent to chunk detectors. Record against-baseline ranges and
present share. A large present share (approaching a third in otherwise past prose)
needs a planned/priced conversion read, not blind conversion. Protect dialogue,
deliberate interior monologue, timeless truths, and direct address. A conversion
handles the entire authorized passage (action/tags, will→would/can→could,
present-perfect→past-perfect) or none of it; never convert declared exceptions
piecemeal. Zero tense findings on a novel is not proof that the baseline was held.
For nonfiction/academic work run report-only `docproof cites` and decide unresolved
citations/cross-references from evidence; missing scaffolding auto-skips its checks.

House numbers spell through one hundred, use `4:00 AM` and `40 percent`. Preserve
quoted facts and detect the book's English variant. Protect author-declared caps,
wordplay/meta-text, dialect, coined names, personified capitals, Scripture/liturgy/
historical quotations. Use the dedicated religious preset for theology; verse
uses poetry-touch and real-word misspellings only. Headings/labels are structural,
not necessarily Word Heading style. An incorrect numbered heading is mechanics.

Produce profile.json (genre, word count, chapter map, tense/person/exceptions,
proper_nouns{enforce,protect}, tics, intent zones, bespoke sweeps, numbers,
comment_budget, reading band). Inspect counts and short context slices, never the
whole extracted book in the coordinator. Number/continuity reader windows preserve
all coverage without loading the manuscript into each planning turn.
