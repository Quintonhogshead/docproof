Spell & Check is a proofread for self-publishing authors: typos, grammar, and the name you spelled two ways — found across your whole novel and handed back as tracked changes you accept or reject in Word. The reading is done by AI (we're upfront about that), the checking is shown as a small adventuring party working through your book, and the whole thing costs less than dinner.
