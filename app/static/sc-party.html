The programme

What's actually happening

Plainly: Spell & Check runs your manuscript through DocProof, the proofreading engine we built for a real publishing house. Each party member is one of its detection systems — the costume changes, the machinery doesn't. The small print under each card names the real system, always.

What AI does: reads your pages to find errors, checks its own proposed fixes with a second AI acting as judge, and writes each change as a tracked change you accept or reject in Word. On the deeper tiers, style thoughts arrive only as comments phrased as questions.

What AI never does: rewrite your voice, make silent changes, keep your manuscript, or train on it. Your file is read to do the job, held 30 days in case you want the free re-run, then deleted.
