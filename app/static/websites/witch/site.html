02Beyond the boundary stones

Welcome to
Everwatch.

A prison-city. An uneasy kingdom.
A secret waiting beneath it all.

An imagined glimpse of Everwatch

A city built to keep you in

Home.
And a prison.

Everwatch is the underground prison-city Gavriel Hall calls home. Beyond its boundary stones is a life he longs for—and an escape he has never dared attempt.

Now, a dead king’s heirs are turning its streets into a battlefield.

The setting / Spoiler-free

The king’s unwanted son

A liar.
A reluctant hero.

Gavriel is a pathological liar who avoids conflict at all costs. On bad days, he records the names of those sacrificed as part of the soul tithe.

On good days, he lets himself pine for Aziz: his best friend, a mysterious outcast with the twin swords of a forgotten hero.

The protagonist / Spoiler-free

A voice beneath the city

She has
an offer.

The witch has been imprisoned below Everwatch longer than even the faerie lords can remember. Her name is a curse. Her sins haunt nightmares.

When Gavriel’s hopes of a new life fall apart, she offers him enough dark magic to fix everything. For a price.
